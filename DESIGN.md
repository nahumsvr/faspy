## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural bridge between institutional enterprise trust and hyper-tactile, contemporary spatial software for embedded factoring and automated liquidity middleware. The emotional core reconciles two traditionally opposing vectors: bank-grade operational precision and physical, touch-inviting warmth. It eliminates sterile, spreadsheet-like corporate debt tooling in favor of an interface that feels tangible, responsive, and effortlessly organic.

The design movement synthesizes **Claymorphism** with **Apple Fluid Material Design**:
- **Inflated Volumetric Geometry:** Surfaces reject pure planar flatness. Panels, cards, and interactive controls feature pillowed physical forms that look soft to the touch, evoking extruded matte silicone, frosted mineral glass, and warm stone.
- **Fluid Translucency & Specular Boundaries:** Elevated floating layers utilize multi-stop background filters (`blur(20px) saturate(180%)`) framed by razor-thin, luminous perimeter rims that capture ambient environmental illumination.
- **Physics-Informed Dynamic Kinetic Response:** Every interaction triggers organic spring damping rather than linear tween transitions. Presses depress into the canvas with soft elastic resistance (`scale(0.97)`), reinforcing direct manipulation of liquidity flows and asset blocks.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-velocity capital routing in deep institutional oceanic blues, paired with visceral functional accents and neutral, chalky clay foundations.

- **Deep Sovereign Blue (`#004977`):** The primary anchor. Carries institutional authority, contractual finality, and foundational layout weight. Applied to root-level actions, primary navigation anchors, and macro-state indicators.
- **Mint Liquidity (`#10B981`):** The secondary conversion engine. Represents instant cash realization, settled factoring transactions, automated disbursements, and positive cash flow velocity. Used with calibrated restraint to signal solvency and success states.
- **Soft Indigo Graph (`#6366F1`):** The tertiary predictive engine. Reserved for automated underwriting models, AI debtor graph scoring, dynamic discount yield curves, and telemetry analysis.
- **Neutral Mineral Foundation:**
  - Base canvas: Pure Chalk (`#F8FAFC`)
  - Recessed trays & gutter wells: Soft Plaster (`#F1F5F9`)
  - Subdued structural strokes: Toned Grout (`#E2E8F0`)
- **Typography & Scrim Contrast Hierarchy:**
  - Deep Ink (`#0F172A`): Primary headings, monetary values, and contractual terms.
  - Slate Medium (`#334155`): Secondary descriptive text, labels, and table metadata.
  - Slate Muted (`#64748B`): Helper annotations, disabled tokens, timestamps, and axis markers.
  - Specular White (`#FFFFFF`): Light sources, inner bevel crests, top-edge highlights, and elevated cards.

## Typography

Typography establishes tension between humanistic, friendly geometric letterforms and strict, tabular algorithmic telemetry.

- **Display & Headings:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans** with negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and tight, compressed vertical rhythm. Large titles emulate physical stamping on dense paper or smooth clay tablets—confident, bold, and modern.
- **Body Text:** Maintained in **Plus Jakarta Sans** at standard weights (`400` and `500`) to guarantee legibility across complex risk disclosures, underwriting audit trails, and multi-tenant ledger views.
- **Data, Currency & Machine Scoring:** Set in **JetBrains Mono**. Factoring ledgers, dynamic yield percentages, interest rate spreads, API payloads, and debtor credit ratings demand fixed-width tabular alignment to ensure zero horizontal drift during real-time data streaming and automated recalculations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt base grid implemented through an elastic, high-breathability fluid container architecture.

- **Grid Systems:**
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column adaptive fluid grid with a maximum structural container width of `1600px`. Gutters sit at `1.5rem` (`24px`) with `2rem` (`32px`) external margins, allowing modular volumetric tiles to distribute naturally.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Sidewall canvas margins are reduced to `1.5rem` (`24px`). Ledger modules consolidate while financial data columns stack into master-detail accordions.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column responsive grid with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and margins. Deep nesting collapses entirely into full-bleed fluid clay sheets stacked along the z-axis.
- **Volumetric Breathing Space:** Because claymorphic elements occupy volumetric visual space via extruded internal and external dual shadows, component gaps must never drop below `space-md` (`16px`). Tight spacing disrupts shadow diffusion and flattens physical depth.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through simulated physical extrusion, internal pillowed highlights, and translucent frosted refractions. Flat, hard-edged drop shadows are strictly avoided.

### 1. The Clay Inflation Model
Every prominent surface has a tactile volume formed by three coordinated lighting layers:
- **Direct Specular Highlight (Light Source from top-left):**
  `inset 3px 3px 6px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`
- **Inverse Ambient Occlusion (Bottom-right bevel):**
  `inset -3px -3px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`
- **Atmospheric Floor Shadow (Soft, diffused grounding):**
  `0 20px 35px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`

### 2. Apple Fluid Translucent Glass Materials
Floating utility bars, global navigation layers, context drawers, and modal sheets sit physically above the clay base:
- **Material Filter:** `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`
- **Surface Fill:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` (or `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.65)` over colored data visuals)
- **Edge Definition:** A hairline rim composed of `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` with an ambient border shadow `0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.

### 3. Depth Hierarchy Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Recessed canvas tone (`#F1F5F9`) with sunken interior fields (`inset 2px 2px 4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`).
- **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Tiles):** Standard clay extrusion (`#FFFFFF`) with base inflation shadows.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Modules & Modals):** Translucent glass sheets hovering over clay surfaces with expanded ambient blur: `0 30px 60px -15px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Tier 3 (Pressed / Engraved):** Scaled down to `0.97` with inverted shadows, removing the outer ambient blur and strengthening the inner inset shadows to visually compress the element into the layout surface.

## Shapes

The design system uses pillowed, organic geometries that eliminate sharp vertices entirely:

- **Global Geometric Scale:** Set to level `3` (Pill-shaped & generous continuous curvature). Standard cards, data panels, and structural dialogs employ broad radiuses ranging from `24px` to `32px` (`1.5rem` - `2rem`).
- **Continuous Tangency (Squircle Curvature):** Corners should leverage CSS `corner-smoothing: 60%` (where browser-supported) or generous border radii to produce smooth, non-mechanical transitions that maintain the extruded clay appearance.
- **Pill Primitives:** Buttons, interactive tags, metric badges, search fields, and slider toggles maintain complete terminal curvature (`border-radius: 9999px`). This contrasts sharply with legacy enterprise software, signaling fluid manipulation and tactile warmth.

- Hybrid Glass Pill: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`.
- Scoring Indicator (`#6366F1`): Emits a subtle breathing glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.3)`.
- Monospaced typography: JetBrains Mono (`label-tag`), 11px uppercase with `0.04em` tracking.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Segmented Control Rails:** Carved-out background wells (`#F1F5F9` with inner occlusion) holding an elevated floating clay toggle (`#FFFFFF` with dual clay shadows) that slides fluidly across states using spring physics (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`).
- **Checkboxes:** Generous `8px` rounded squircle corners, soft inset resting base, and animated Mint (`#10B981`) inflated fill with a bright white SVG checkmark upon activation.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Invoice Factoring Velocity Slider:** Tactile pill thumb (`36px` diameter) with heavy clay extrusion, resting on a sunken progress rail filled with vibrant Mint (`#10B981`). Real-time calculated payout numbers snap overhead within an Apple-style frosted tooltip HUD.
- **Dynamic Ledger Data Rows:** Floating grouped horizontal cards separated by `space-sm` (`8px`) instead of flat table dividers. On hover, rows lift slightly (`translateY(-2px)`) with an intensified specular rim.
